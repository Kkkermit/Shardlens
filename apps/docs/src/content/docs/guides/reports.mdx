A report is a short summary Shardlens posts to a Discord channel on a schedule, so your team sees the numbers without
opening a dashboard. It goes to whichever channel you like, through that channel's webhook.

## What a report says

```text
📊 Daily report · Ticket Bot
Wed 12 Nov (Europe/London)

Commands run: 12,431 (▲ 8.2% on the day before)
Unique users: 3,204 (412 new)
Failed: 0.4% · Missed the 3-second deadline: 0.1% · p95 response: 412 ms
Servers: 1,204 (+12 joined, −3 left)

Top commands
1. /ticket · 4,210
2. /help · 2,100
3. /close · 1,870

Open in Shardlens
```

A weekly report covers the seven days before the day it is sent, and shows the average number of unique users a day.
Reports contain **counts only**. No user is ever named in one, in any mode.

## Set one up

<Steps>

1. **Create a webhook in the channel you want**

   In Discord: open the channel's settings, then **Integrations → Webhooks → New Webhook → Copy Webhook URL**. The
   channel you create it in is the channel reports arrive in.

2. **Create the report**

   In the Shardlens dashboard, open **Reports** (you need to be signed in with Discord as the app owner or a team
   member). Give it a name, choose **Daily** or **Weekly**, the hour, and paste the webhook URL.

3. **Try it**

   Press **Send now**. The report for the last finished day or week appears in your channel straight away.

</Steps>

Times are in the time zone from **Settings → General**. You can have up to 10 reports, so different channels can get
different schedules.

## Good to know

- **Discord webhooks only.** The URL must be `https://discord.com/api/webhooks/…` (`discordapp.com`,
  `canary.discord.com` and `ptb.discord.com` work too). Anything else is refused, so the collector can't be pointed at
  other systems.
- **Unique users are counted per UTC day.** In another time zone, a daily report shows the UTC day that overlaps your
  day the most.
- **From the API.** `POST /v1/reports` takes the same fields as the form. `hour` is 0 to 23 in your time zone, and
  `weekday` is 1 for Monday to 7 for Sunday.
- **Stored encrypted.** The webhook URL is encrypted with `SHARDLENS_SETTINGS_KEY` and only ever shown masked.
- **One attempt per period.** If Discord refuses a report (a deleted webhook, for example), the reason is shown in
  the **Last sent** column and the report is tried again at its next scheduled time, not every few minutes.
- **If the collector was down** at the scheduled hour, the report is sent as soon as it is back that day.

<Aside type="tip">
  Want to be told the moment something breaks, rather than the next morning? That is what [alerts](../alerts/) are
  for. Want the numbers in your own web dashboard instead? See [Use your own dashboard](../your-own-dashboard/).
</Aside>
