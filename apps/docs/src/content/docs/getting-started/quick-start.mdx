You need a bot on discord.js 14.12 or later, or 15, on Node 22.12 or later. If your bot already uses MongoDB (Atlas,
including its free tier, or your own server), Shardlens keeps everything there: no server, Docker, domain or keys.
No MongoDB? [Run the collector on a server](#without-mongodb-run-the-collector-on-a-server) instead.

Or let your coding agent set it up: copy the prompt and paste it with your bot's project open.

<CopyPrompt prompt={install} path="install.md" what="adding Shardlens to your bot" />

<Steps>

1. **Install two packages in your bot**

   <Tabs syncKey="pkg">
     <TabItem label="npm">`npm install @shardlens/discordjs @shardlens/collector`</TabItem>
     <TabItem label="pnpm">`pnpm add @shardlens/discordjs @shardlens/collector`</TabItem>
     <TabItem label="yarn">`yarn add @shardlens/discordjs @shardlens/collector`</TabItem>
     <TabItem label="bun">`bun add @shardlens/discordjs @shardlens/collector`</TabItem>
   </Tabs>

2. **Add one line, with the connection string your bot already has**

   <Code code={mongodb} lang="ts" title="bot.ts" mark={7} />

   Use whatever your `.env` calls it (`MONGODB_URI`, `MONGO_URL`, …). Shardlens adds its own collections, all
   prefixed `shardlens_`, in the database the string names, beside your bot's.

3. **Start your bot and run a command**

   Events are stored from inside the bot. Every process of a sharded bot writes to the same database.

4. **Open the dashboard**

   In your bot's folder, on your computer or the bot's host:

   ```sh
   npx shardlens dashboard
   ```

   It finds the connection string in your `.env`, serves the dashboard on this machine and prints a link that signs
   you in. Open it: the Overview counts your command within seconds.

</Steps>

<Aside type="tip">
  Only the `Guilds` intent is needed. Shardlens never reads message content, and user IDs never leave your bot: they
  are hashed in process before anything is stored.
</Aside>

To keep the dashboard open for good, at your own address, serve it from the bot or from a small host: see
[On your MongoDB](../../self-hosting/mongodb/#keep-the-dashboard-running).

## Without MongoDB: run the collector on a server

The collector stores events in PostgreSQL and serves the dashboard at your domain. You need a server with Docker and a
domain pointing at it.

<Steps>

1. **Start the collector**

   `shardlens init` writes `docker-compose.yml`, a `Caddyfile`, a `.env` with generated secrets and keys, and a
   `bot.env` holding the two settings your bot needs.

   ```sh
   npx shardlens init --domain stats.example.com --app-id 123456789012345678
   cd shardlens && docker compose up -d
   ```

   Caddy gets a certificate for your domain, the collector creates its tables, and the dashboard is served at
   `https://stats.example.com`.

2. **Install the SDK in your bot**

   <Tabs syncKey="pkg">
     <TabItem label="npm">`npm install @shardlens/discordjs`</TabItem>
     <TabItem label="pnpm">`pnpm add @shardlens/discordjs`</TabItem>
     <TabItem label="yarn">`yarn add @shardlens/discordjs`</TabItem>
     <TabItem label="bun">`bun add @shardlens/discordjs`</TabItem>
   </Tabs>

3. **Add three lines to your bot**

   <Code code={quickStart} lang="ts" title="bot.ts" />

   Copy the two lines from `bot.env` (`SHARDLENS_URL` and `SHARDLENS_INGEST_KEY`) to wherever your bot reads its
   settings, then delete `bot.env`.

4. **Open the dashboard**

   Go to your domain and sign in with your read key (`SHARDLENS_READ_KEY` in `.env`), or with Discord once you have
   [set up Discord sign-in](../../self-hosting/docker-compose/#discord-sign-in).

</Steps>

Already have a dashboard of your own, or want none? [Choose your setup](../choose-your-setup/) compares the options.
