This page is **generated from the metric registry** in `@shardlens/schema`, the same list the collector's rollups use.

- **Counters** are summed into minute, hour and day rollups.
- **Histograms** use 40 fixed buckets on a logarithmic scale from 1 ms to 60 s, so percentiles can be merged across
  shards and time ranges. A percentile is reported as the upper edge of the bucket it falls in: up to a third above
  the true value, never below it. Anything slower than 60 s lands in the last bucket and reads as about 80 s.
- **Gauges** are reported by every shard and process. Totals (`shard.guilds`, `memory.*`, `cache.*`) are added up
  across them; latencies (`shard.ping_ms`, `eventloop.p99_ms`) are averaged, with the worst kept as the maximum.
